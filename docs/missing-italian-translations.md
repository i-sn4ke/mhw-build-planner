Mancano anche le descrizioni generali e gli effetti del livello 1 per queste dieci abilità.

- **Clutch Claw Boost**: Clutch claw weapon attacks wound monsters easier, and have a better chance of dropping slinger ammo.
- **Inheritance**: Removes the skill level cap for the skill secrets.
- **Transcendance**: Grants True Razor Sharp/Spare Shot. Health/Stamina +100 at the start of a quest or after fainting. (Effect doesn't stack.)
- **Fun Fright's Gift**: Increases odds of getting special Fun Fright rewards. (No effect when joining mid-quest.)
- **Fun Fright's Gratitude**: Greatly increases odds of getting special Fun Fright rewards. (No effect when joining mid-quest.)
- **Sizzling Gift**: Increases odds of getting special Sizzling Spice rewards. (No effect when joining mid-quest.)
- **Sizzling Gratitude**: Greatly increases odds of getting special Sizzling Spice rewards. (No effect when joining mid-quest.)
- **Coldproof**: Nullifies the effects of cold weather.
- **Provoker**: Increases the attention drawn when attacking a monster.
- **Safe Landing**: Allows you to quickly recover from heavy attacks that blow you back when you have your weapon unsheathed.
